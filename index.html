Academic Background & Focus

I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Applied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ACSAI) from Sapienza University of Rome with a final grade of 110/110, completing an undergraduate curriculum taught entirely in English.

I completed my curricular internship and bachelor's thesis at the National Institute of Geophysics and Volcanology (INGV), engineering Seismic Viewer: an interactive 4D desktop application for spatio-temporal seismic catalogue visualization, spatial clustering, and automated geological fault plane estimation.

I am currently pursuing my Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (AIRO) at Sapienza (started in 2026), focusing on multi-agent systems, local LLMs, computer vision with YOLO, and concurrent robotic control. I also completed technical programs with IBM and Ericsson, and achieved the top score in the TeraBebras competition.

Thesis & Internship
INGV (National Institute of Geophysics and Volcanology) & Sapienza

Designed and engineered Seismic Viewer, an interactive 4D desktop application for spatio-temporal seismic data visualization. Developed a Python scientific backend for spatial clustering and automated geological fault plane estimation. Built a high-performance WebGL 3D rendering frontend with a custom binary streaming protocol achieving an 80% data reduction compared to JSON for fluid framerates on large seismic datasets (Campi Flegrei).
